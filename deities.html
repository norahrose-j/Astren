THE DAGHDA


The Daghda is the head of the Astran pantheon. He is the god of the weather and the crops and is denoted by a bubbling cauldron and a muted sage colour. As the head of the pantheon, his worship is common, though most frequent amongst farmers and commonpeople.

Followers can benefit from the Nature or Trickery domains.

MORRIGAN


Morrigan is the goddes of war and is denoted by three crossed spears over a crest. She is favoured by warriors of all kinds, particularly by generals and strategists. She is particularly favoured during times of war, though there are those whose worship of her persists into times of peace.

Followers can benefit from the War domain.

LUGH


Lugh is the goddess of arts, travel and commerce. She is denoted by a pair of slender hands, frequently on a gold coin. Lugh is most popular among merchants, traders and craftsmen, though it is common to offer a prayer to Lugh before embarking on a journey of any kind.

Followers can benefit from the Knowledge, Light or Life domains.

DUNATIS


Dunatis is the god of mountains and peaks. His worshippers are mostly found in and around the Drakensberg and Altai mountain ranges. His followers tend to be strong and his worship involves physical feats.

Followers can benefit from the Nature or Tempest domains.

MATH MATHONWY


Math Mathonwy is the god of magic and the arcane. He is denoted by an antlered stage and indigo stars. Worship of Math Mathnowy is popular with arcanists as well as the faithful, making him a popular choice for the many scholars and researchers in Astren.

Followers can benefit from the Knowledge or Arcana domains.

MANANNAN MAC LIR


Manannan Mac Lir is the god of oceans and the sea. He is denoted by a cresting wave and the colours of the ocean. He is most worshipped by sailors those living along a coast. It is very common to offer prayers to Manannan Mac Lir before setting sail or travelling by water.

Followers can benefit from the Nature or Tempest domains.

MACHA


Macha is the goddess of sovereignty and leadership. She is denoted by a crown of laurels and an eight-pointed star. As the goddess of leadership, Macha has come to be representative of the Empire and her worship is popular among the various leaders of the continent.

Followers can benefit from the Order or Peace domains.

SILVANUS


Silvanus is the god of nature and forests. He is denoted by an oak tree and strongly associated with deep greens. Seen as the protector of the natural world, he is favoured by druids and rangers and his worship is most common in the forested regions of Astren.

Followers can benefit from the Nature domain.

OGHMA


Oghma is the god of knowledge and writing, he is denoted by a quill and ink. He is said to be the creator of language and writing and serves as the scribe of the gods. He is frequently a favoured deity for scholars and researchers.

Followers can benefit from the Knowledge domain.

BELENUS


Belenus is the god of the sun, light and warmth. He is denoted by a radiant sun and frequently associated with warm yellows. He is revered by many as an antithesis to evil and is believed to grant powers which aid in fighting it. His worship can be found across Astren, though he is more prominent in the south.

Followers can benefit from the Life or Light domains.

NUADA


Nuada is the god of war and warriors. He is denoted by a silver hand, which is said to represent the God's own prosthetic hand. He is favoured by soldiers, mercenaries and fighters of all kinds. Prayers to Nuada are commonly made before or during fights or battles of all kinds.

Followers can benefit from the War domain.

BRIGANTIA


Brigantia is the goddess of livestock and fertility. She is denoted most commonly by a bull's skull, though many animals are used to symbolize her. She is a favoured deity for farmers and cultivators, making her worship common in rural areas but infrequent in cities and urban centers.

Followers can benefit from the Life or Nature domains.

ARAWN


Arawn is the goddess of life and death. She is denoted by an overgrown skull and other markers of decay. She is said to wield a dual-nature staff, used to kill and to heal. Prayers to Arawn are frequently made at births, deaths and to request aid in healing or recovery from illness or injury.

Followers can benefit from the Life or Death domains.

GOIBNIU


Goibniu is the god of smiths and healing. He is denoted by a crossed hammer and sword, frequently overlaid on a golden crest. Goibniu is popular among blacksmiths and other craftsmen, though it is not uncommon for soldiers or other combattants to offer prayers to Goibniu.

Followers can benefit from the Forge or Knowledge domains.

CAILLEACH


Cailleach is the goddess of winter and old age. She is denoted by a flying raven and strongly associated with the bright, cool colours of winter. Prayers to Cailleach are frequent during the winter

Followers can benefit from the Twilight or Tempest domains.
