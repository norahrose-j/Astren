Glade Wardens

Eutropia Archon

Eutropia Archon is a highly proficient cleric of Silvanus. She is the longest serving Warden of the Glades, and is an incredibly respected figure both within the Grey Glades and beyond. In addition to being the Warden of Infina and its surrounding area, she is also a teacher of the faith. She is a source of knowledge and power for all followers of Silvanus, who travel from across Astren to the Glades in search of her wisdom and counsel.

Eutropia is an eladrin, though she is able to shift between seasons, she is most typically in a spring form. Because of her elven heritage she appears to be in her 40s, though her true age is likely close to 300. She has long and curly straw-coloured hair, tan skin and bright blue eyes, and is generally seen wearing a crown of ivy and deep green robes. She carries a staff of oak, which is gnarled and shaped as though it were the root of a tree.
Thanks to her connection to Silvanus, Eutropia is able to cast the following cleric spells, as a 10th level spellcaster.

Cantrips (at will): Guidance, Mending, Spare the Dying, Resistance, Light, Druidcraft
1st Level (4 slots): Animal Friendship, Speak with Animals, Ceremony, Cure Wounds, Purify Food & Drink, Command
2nd Level (3 slots): Lesser Restoration, Calm Emotions, Gentle Repose
3rd Level (3 slots): Barkskin, Spike Growth, Dispel Magic, Revivify, Water Walk
4th Level (3 slots): Control Water, Stone Shape, Guardian of Faith
5th Level (2 slots): Plant Growth, Wind Wall, Greater Restoration, Holy Weapon

Alton Hart

Alton Hart is the Warden in Omene and its surrounding regions. He is ranger and is highly familiar with swamp environment of the Glades. Alton is in his 30s. He has shoulder-length red hair and a short beard, pale skin and green eyes. He is generally seen wearing leathers, with a cloak and twin shortswords. He is always accompanied by his companion, Bruce, a Glade Panther.

Alton is the youngest Warden and was only recently elected after Neristina Archon's death. He was chosen because of his strong connection to the land, and his relationship with Neristina before her death.
Because of his connection to the land, Alton can cast the following spells as a first level spellcaster.

1st Level (2 Slots): Hunter's Mark, Detect Magic, Cure Wounds

Zinnia Hale

Zinnia Hale is the Warden in Idae and its surrounding regions. She is one of the few wardens without a strong magical affinity, but is an accomplished student and researcher of the region. Zinnia is a human woman in her 60s, she has white hair and deep brown eyes. She is generally seen wearing mail or other heavier armours and carrying a large Claymore on her back. She is always accompanied by her familiar, a Kingfisher named Rex.

Zinnia is a trained Runesmith, and as such is able to use her knowledge of ancient runes to empower her sword with magical abilities. She is an accomplished smith, beyond Runesmithing, and is a mentor and teacher for many who aspire to the profession. As part of her studies, she has gleaned some magical knowledge and is able to cast the following spells as a first level spellcaster:

Cantrips (At Will): Elementalism, Message
1st Level (1/Day): Find Familiar
